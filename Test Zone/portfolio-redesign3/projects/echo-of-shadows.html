About this project

A stealth game where light is the main mechanic. Stay in the shadows to slip past the guards; step into the light and they'll see you.

Key features

  • Light-based visibility: How visible you are depends on the light falling on you, so the level's lighting is the stealth system.
  • Guard AI: Guards patrol, investigate noises and give chase, reacting to what they can actually see.
  • Multithreaded checks: Visibility and AI checks run in parallel off the main thread, keeping the frame rate steady with many guards.
  • Lighting: Baked and real-time lighting combined, so it looks good without costing too much.

Challenges and what I learned

Visibility checks are expensive: every guard asking "can I see the player?" every frame adds up fast. Moving that work onto worker threads, and only checking guards that could plausibly see you, kept the game smooth.
