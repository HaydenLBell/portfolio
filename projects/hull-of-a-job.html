About this project

Most games set under the sea are about danger. This one isn't. You're a contractor sent down to clean and repair submarines and other vessels, and the satisfaction comes from leaving each one spotless and working again.

It's designed to be low-stimulation: slow, steady tasks, a clear sense of progress, and nothing rushing you. We've built a playable demo level, and the game is in early alpha.

Key features

  • Cleaning: Mop away grime and pick up rubbish as you work your way through the vessel.
  • Repairs: A handheld repair tool for fixing damaged parts of the vessel.
  • Job progress: A progress bar tracks each job up to 100%, so you always know how much is left.
  • Look and lighting: A cartoon-styled underwater world that stops short of toony, with moody lighting inspired by Deus Ex: Human Revolution.

Challenges and what I learned

Lighting carries a lot of the mood, so an early job was moving the project from Unity's built-in render pipeline to URP, which gave more control over how the vessels are lit. The game is built on a first-person controller framework, so our time goes into the cleaning and repair systems rather than movement.

The challenge now is variety. The demo level works, but cleaning and repairing can start to feel samey, so the next step is giving each vessel and task its own character: different kinds of mess, different repairs, and vessels that look and feel distinct.
